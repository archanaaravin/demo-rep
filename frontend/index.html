Our Mission

Reduce preventable road fatalities by equipping drivers, emergency responders, and municipal planners with predictive spatial intelligence.

Core Vision

Transforming urban road networks to be proactive rather than reactive — forecasting accidents the same way meteorologists forecast storms.

Government Impact
