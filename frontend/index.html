Our Mission

Reduce preventable road fatalities by equipping drivers, emergency responders, and municipal planners with predictive spatial intelligence.

Core Vision

Transforming urban road networks to be proactive rather than reactive — forecasting accidents the same way meteorologists forecast storms.

Citizen Reporting

Empowering everyday citizens to drop pin-point hazard reports — with photo evidence — directly onto the live telemetry map for instant AI validation and dispatch.

Government Impact

Providing city authorities with data-driven infrastructure prioritization and automated resource allocation to save lives efficiently.
